# Product and design principles

**Audience:** owner/operator of a small field-service business.

**Job to be done:** track a customer request through completed and paid work, and see whether the job made money.

**Design:** quiet neutral surfaces, deliberate typography, ample but efficient spacing, readable contrast, polished mobile layouts, restrained motion, obvious next actions, no ornamental dashboards. Inspired by Apple and Wealthsimple interaction quality, not a visual clone.

**4H1W gate:** What problem? Why now? Who benefits? How does it work and fail? When is it worth shipping?

**Verification:** check empty, loading, error, keyboard, narrow viewport, realistic amounts, and permission boundaries. A clickable demo is not a production system.

**Definition of done:** complete flow with representative synthetic data; totals reconcile; no dead-end controls; mobile usable; known limitations documented.
